# Limitations

OpenDefendable is a truth surface, not a certification authority.

## Current limitations

- Runtime modules are verified with limitations, not production-cleared.
- DefendableCloud remains under repair for server-side action API proof.
- DefendableDocs broader claim alignment remains under repair.
- DefendableRouter public utility and Cloud integration remain unverified from public surface evidence.
- Client pilots are held.
- Production is not cleared.
- External SaaS enforcement is not cleared.
- Certification, insurance, and blockchain anchoring are not claimed.

## Hash limitation

SHA-256 hashes published in this ecosystem establish content-integrity linkage only.
They do not independently prove authorship, owner approval, certification,
insurance coverage, blockchain anchoring, or production clearance.
