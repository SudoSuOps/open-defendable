# Continuity Protocol

Purpose: preserve a clear chain from starter to backup to coverage.

Inputs:
- role assignments
- roster health evidence
- owner decisions

Outputs:
- continuity assignment
- coverage label

Pass/fail rules:
- starter must not silently collapse into backup state
- backup must carry continuity assignment when activated

Status label:
- `VERIFIED_AS_REPAIRED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`

Limitations:
- applies to the audited prototype chain only
